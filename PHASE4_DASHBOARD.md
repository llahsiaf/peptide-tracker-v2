# BioStack PRO — Phase 4 Dashboard

Phase 4 adds a Today-first personal tracking dashboard on top of the Phase 3 vial lifecycle and schedule engine.

## Included
- Today tab as the app landing screen.
- Weekly date strip with previous/next week navigation.
- Selected-date schedule and injection log view.
- 7-day upcoming schedule preview.
- Inventory/freezer/lifecycle summary tiles.
- 30-day personal tracking analytics based only on local tracker data.
- Most frequently logged peptides (descriptive only).
- Quick navigation to Inventory for logging an injection.

## Data boundary
Analytics are descriptive calculations from local schedule/history/inventory data. No medical dosing or treatment recommendation logic is introduced here.
